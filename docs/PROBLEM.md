# Problem

Merchants lose recoverable revenue when payment failures are handled with generic, repeated, or poorly timed follow-ups. The problem is particularly acute for UPI mandates, subscription renewals, and high-value B2B receivables, where the right recovery action depends on why the payment failed.

Recoup helps a merchant's Revenue Operations or Payments Operations team process a batch of failures consistently. It identifies the failure type, applies an explicit recovery policy with hard stopping rules, records the cost of every action, and makes the eventual outcome reviewable.

## Non-goals

- Initiating real payment-gateway retries
- Sending real SMS, WhatsApp, or voice messages
- Managing merchant accounts or tenants
- Replacing a merchant's compliance programme
